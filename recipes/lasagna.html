Lasagna

Description of Lasagna

Lasagna is a classic Italian oven-baked casserole made by stacking wide, flat pasta sheets with alternating layers of rich sauces, meats, cheeses, and vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Marinara Sauce
  • Cheese
  • Regular lasagna noodles

Steps

  1. Brown the beef and onion.
  2. Prepare the baking dish and assemble the meat sauce.
  3. Layer the lasagna.
  4. Bake the lasagna.
  5. Sprinkle with the remaining mozzarella and finish baking.
  6. Cool the lasagna.
